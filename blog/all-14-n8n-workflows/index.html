Most n8n users start with one workflow. But the real power of n8n comes from connecting multiple automations into a coherent system. I built 14 production workflows for a live business and documented each one.

Here is a complete guide to every workflow in the Starter Pack.

1. Email Triage Bot

Scans your inbox every morning, classifies emails with AI, and delivers a prioritized digest to your messaging app.

  • Checks unread emails from the last 24 hours
  • Classifies each as URGENT, REPLY TODAY, FYI, or JUNK
  • Sends a clean digest to Telegram or Slack
  • Optional: marks emails as read after sending digest

This is the workflow most people start with. I have a free guide that walks through building it step by step.

2. Invoice Automation

Monitors an inbox or folder for incoming invoices, extracts key details, categorizes them, and logs them in a spreadsheet.

  • Scans Gmail or folder for invoice emails
  • Uses AI to extract vendor name, amount, due date
  • Appends to Google Sheets or Airtable
  • Optional: creates reminders for upcoming due dates

Another free guide covers this workflow in detail.

3. Lead Qualification

Takes inbound leads from forms or emails, scores them based on predefined criteria, and routes high-priority leads to your CRM or notification channel.

  • Receives lead data from web forms, email, or API
  • Evaluates against scoring criteria (budget, timeline, fit)
  • Routes qualified leads to CRM or Slack
  • Sends automated acknowledgment to unqualified leads

4. Social Media Auto-Posting

Takes content from a calendar or RSS feed and publishes it to multiple social platforms on a schedule.

  • Connects Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and other platforms
  • Schedules posts at optimal times
  • Detects and removes duplicate content
  • Tracks post performance in a spreadsheet

5. Client Onboarding

Automates the welcome sequence when a new client signs up, including welcome emails, document requests, and calendar booking.

  • Triggers on new client record in CRM
  • Sends welcome email with onboarding checklist
  • Requests necessary documents via form
  • Schedules kick-off meeting on client calendar

6. CRM Contact Enrichment

Takes new contact records and appends publicly available data from enrichment APIs.

  • Monitors CRM for new or updated contacts
  • Enriches with company data from public APIs
  • Appends social profiles and firmographics
  • Flags incomplete records for manual review

7. Weekly Report Generator

Aggregates metrics from multiple sources and compiles them into a summary sent weekly.

  • Pulls data from Google Analytics, Stripe, support tools
  • Computes key metrics: revenue, traffic, conversion
  • Formats summary as email or PDF
  • Sends to specified recipients every Monday

8. Task Dependency Tracker

Links tasks across different tools and alerts when a dependency is completed or blocked.

  • Monitors tasks in project management tools
  • Tracks dependencies between tasks
  • Alerts when prerequisites are completed
  • Flags tasks stuck on missing dependencies

9. Meeting Note Summarizer

Collects meeting notes from multiple sources and uses AI to generate structured summaries with action items.

  • Collects notes from email, calendar, or shared docs
  • Sends notes to AI for summarization
  • Extracts action items and assigns owners
  • Sends summary to meeting participants

10. Payment Reconciliation

Matches incoming payments to outstanding invoices automatically, flagging mismatches for review.

  • Monitors Stripe, PayPal, or bank feed for payments
  • Matches payments to invoices by amount or reference
  • Flags partial payments and discrepancies
  • Updates invoice status in spreadsheet or CRM

11. Slack Notification Router

Collects alerts from multiple sources and routes them to the right Slack channel based on type, priority, and team.

  • Receives webhooks from multiple services
  • Categorizes alerts by type and priority
  • Routes to appropriate Slack channels
  • Aggregates low-priority alerts into daily digest

12. Customer Feedback Analyzer

Collects reviews, support tickets, and survey responses, analyzes sentiment, and categorizes feedback themes.

  • Aggregates feedback from reviews, email, and surveys
  • Classifies sentiment as positive, neutral, or negative
  • Categorizes themes (product, support, pricing)
  • Compiles results into weekly summary

13. Data Sync Pipeline

Synchronizes data between two systems, handling transforms and errors.

  • Defines source and destination systems
  • Transforms data between formats
  • Handles new records and updates
  • Logs errors and retries failed transfers

14. API Rate Limiter

Protects your integrations by throttling API requests to stay within provider limits, with automatic retry on 429 responses.

  • Wraps any API call with rate-limiting logic
  • Tracks request counts and resets
  • Queues requests and processes when allowed
  • Alerts when limits are approaching
