Best n8n Workflow Templates for Small Business

Published July 2026 · MadeByAiMe

If you run a small business, startup, or freelance operation, you are probably doing a lot of repetitive digital tasks by hand. Copy-pasting between apps. Manually sorting emails. Creating the same invoices every week. Logging data from forms into spreadsheets.

n8n is an open-source workflow automation tool that lets you connect apps and automate tasks without writing code. You can build these automations yourself, or you can use pre-built workflow templates.

Below are the 14 most practical n8n workflow templates for small business, based on what real operators actually need. Each one solves a specific, common problem.

1. Email Triage Bot

Sort your inbox automatically. Classifies emails as URGENT, REPLY TODAY, FYI, or JUNK using AI, then sends you a clean daily digest to Telegram or Slack so you only deal with what matters.

Apps: Gmail, OpenAI, Telegram/Slack · Level: Easy

2. Invoice Automation

When an invoice arrives by email, extract the vendor name, amount, and due date. Create a record in your spreadsheet or accounting app, and track whether it has been paid.

Apps: Gmail, Google Sheets/OpenAI · Level: Easy

3. Lead Qualification

New leads fill out a form or email a message. This workflow scores the lead based on your criteria, routes high-value leads to your CRM, and sends a Slack notification when a hot lead arrives.

Apps: Webhook/Email, OpenAI, HubSpot/Pipedrive, Slack · Level: Medium

4. Weekly Reporting

Aggregates data from your various tools (sales, website, email, social) and generates a weekly summary report delivered straight to your inbox every Monday morning.

Apps: Multiple APIs · Level: Medium

5. Social Media Cross-Post

Write one post and publish it to Twitter, LinkedIn, and any other platform simultaneously. Saves you from writing the same content three times.

Apps: Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook · Level: Easy

6. Calendar Sync

Keep two calendar services in sync. Detect conflicts, send reminders ahead of meetings, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Apps: Google Calendar, Outlook · Level: Easy

7. Google Sheets Logger

Capture form submissions, webhook events, or user data into a Google Sheet in real-time. Useful for lead capture, survey results, or any data collection.

Apps: Webhook, Google Sheets · Level: Easy

8. Slack Notifier

Get structured notifications in your Slack channels when important events happen in any connected service. Order completions, new signups, support tickets, system alerts.

Apps: Multiple, Slack · Level: Easy

9. Data Enrichment

Take a list of contacts or companies and enrich them with publicly available data. Add job titles, company details, or social profiles to your CRM records.

Apps: Multiple APIs · Level: Medium

10. PDF Generator

Take structured data and generate PDF documents. Use this for invoices, reports, quotes, or any document that needs a professional layout.

Apps: Multiple, PDF generation · Level: Medium

11. Task Automation

Monitor tasks across platforms, update their status when conditions change, and notify the relevant people. Useful for project management workflows.

Apps: Multiple, Notion/Trello · Level: Medium

12. Webhook Receiver

A central webhook receiver that listens for incoming webhooks from any service and routes them to the appropriate downstream action. The foundation of many automation chains.

Apps: Any webhook source · Level: Easy

13. Scheduled Report Email

Sends automated email reports on a configurable schedule. Send daily summaries, weekly digests, or monthly reports with custom content and formatting.

Apps: Email, Schedule trigger · Level: Easy

14. Multi-step Approval

Implements an approval workflow with email notifications and escalation rules. Send requests for approval, track responses, and escalate if no one responds in time.

Apps: Email, Slack · Level: Medium

How to Get Started

Each workflow is a JSON file. To use one:

  • Open your n8n instance (self-hosted for free, or approximately $24/mo on n8n Cloud)
  • Import the workflow JSON file through the n8n editor
  • Connect your API credentials for the services you want to use
  • Activate the workflow and run it
